03JULIA'S STORY

Julia Charaf is a certified personal trainer, nutrition coach, corrective exercise specialist, and strength & conditioning coach, backed by a Biology degree from the University of Washington and hands-on clinical experience as a certified medical assistant.

At the University of Washington, Julia worked directly with D1 UW Huskies Football players, building programs to bring elite athletes back from winter break shredded and explosive for spring ball. Before launching her own practice, she honed her craft at a professional personal training organization, an environment that demanded she continuously push her knowledge of how the human body functions and adapts. That relentless pursuit hasn't stopped.

Having competed in sports at a high level, Julia understands what it takes to push past barriers, physically and mentally. That competitive edge, paired with clinical precision, creates a coaching experience that is both results-driven and deeply intelligent.
